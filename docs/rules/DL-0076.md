# DL-0076 · Privileged Entra ID role given to a service principal by an application

**ATT&CK:** [T1098.003](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1098/003/) · **Tactic:** privilege-escalation · **Severity:** critical · **Data:** `entra_audit`

"Add member to role" where the target is a service principal, the role is privileged (Global Administrator, Privileged Role Administrator, Application or Cloud Application Administrator, Exchange Administrator...) and the change was made by an application, not a person. In the October 2026 ConnectWise supply chain compromise (Storm-0940), ConnectWise Cloud Management service principals added themselves to Exchange Administrator in about fifty tenants and, in one, to Global Administrator, five minutes before planting secrets in other apps. Humans assign roles through the portal; an app granting roles to an app is the attacker using permissions such as RoleManagement.ReadWrite.Directory that a vendor was trusted with.

## Cortex XDR (XQL)

```sql
config case_sensitive = false
| dataset = msft_azure_ad_audit_raw
| filter activityDisplayName != null
| alter aud_operation = activityDisplayName, aud_initiator_app = json_extract_scalar(initiatedBy, "$.app.displayName"), aud_initiator_user = json_extract_scalar(initiatedBy, "$.user.userPrincipalName"), aud_target_type = json_extract_scalar(targetResources, "$[0].type"), aud_target_name = json_extract_scalar(targetResources, "$[0].displayName"), aud_targets = to_string(targetResources), aud_user_agent = arrayindex(regextract(to_string(additionalDetails), "User-Agent\\\",\\\"value\\\":\\\"([^\\\"]*)"), 0)
| filter ((aud_operation in ("add member to role", "add eligible member to role") and aud_target_type = "serviceprincipal") and aud_initiator_app ~= "(?i).+") and (aud_targets contains "Global Administrator" or aud_targets contains "Company Administrator" or aud_targets contains "Privileged Role Administrator" or aud_targets contains "Privileged Authentication Administrator" or aud_targets contains "Application Administrator" or aud_targets contains "Cloud Application Administrator" or aud_targets contains "Exchange Administrator" or aud_targets contains "SharePoint Administrator" or aud_targets contains "Security Administrator" or aud_targets contains "Hybrid Identity Administrator" or aud_targets contains "User Administrator" or aud_targets contains "Partner Tier2 Support" or aud_targets contains "Directory Writers")
| fields _time, aud_operation, aud_initiator_app, aud_initiator_user, aud_target_type, aud_target_name, aud_user_agent, aud_targets
| sort desc _time
| limit 1000
```

## Splunk (SPL, Sysmon)

```

| eval aud_operation=operationName, aud_category=category, aud_result='properties.result', aud_initiator_app='properties.initiatedBy.app.displayName', aud_initiator_app_id='properties.initiatedBy.app.appId', aud_initiator_user='properties.initiatedBy.user.userPrincipalName', aud_target_type=mvindex('properties.targetResources{}.type',0), aud_target_name=mvindex('properties.targetResources{}.displayName',0), aud_targets=mvjoin(mvappend('properties.targetResources{}.displayName','properties.targetResources{}.modifiedProperties{}.newValue')," | "), aud_user_agent=mvindex('properties.additionalDetails{}.value',mvfind('properties.additionalDetails{}.key',"User-Agent"))
| where (((lower(aud_operation)="add member to role" OR lower(aud_operation)="add eligible member to role") AND lower(aud_target_type)="serviceprincipal") AND match(aud_initiator_app, "(?i).+")) AND (match(aud_targets, "(?i)Global\\ Administrator") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Company\\ Administrator") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Privileged\\ Role\\ Administrator") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Privileged\\ Authentication\\ Administrator") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Application\\ Administrator") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Cloud\\ Application\\ Administrator") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Exchange\\ Administrator") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)SharePoint\\ Administrator") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Security\\ Administrator") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Hybrid\\ Identity\\ Administrator") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)User\\ Administrator") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Partner\\ Tier2\\ Support") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Directory\\ Writers"))
| table _time aud_operation aud_initiator_app aud_initiator_user aud_target_type aud_target_name aud_user_agent aud_targets

```

## Triage

1. Compare initiator.app with target.name. The same app on both sides is a self-assignment, the exact Storm-0940 pattern, and should be treated as an active intrusion.
2. Look at what the service principal did in the next hour, above all new credentials on other apps (DL-0077) and new high-privilege Graph permissions (DL-0079).
3. Remove the role, disable the service principal (do not delete it, you need its configuration as evidence) and revoke its credentials. Existing app tokens stay valid until they expire, up to about 90 minutes.

## Known false positives

- Identity governance or PIM automation that assigns roles through its own service principal. It uses one known app and a ticketed workflow.
