# DL-0079 · Tenant-wide Microsoft Graph or Exchange permission granted to an app

**ATT&CK:** [T1098.003](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1098/003/), [T1528](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1528/) · **Tactic:** privilege-escalation · **Severity:** high · **Data:** `entra_audit`

"Add app role assignment to service principal" (application permission, admin consent) for a permission that controls the whole tenant: RoleManagement.ReadWrite.Directory, AppRoleAssignment.ReadWrite.All, Application.ReadWrite.All, Directory.ReadWrite.All, Mail.ReadWrite / Mail.Read, Files.ReadWrite.All, Sites.FullControl.All or Exchange full_access_as_app. With any of the first three an app can promote itself to Global Administrator; Microsoft assessed that Storm-0940 likely abused Directory.ReadWrite.All granted to the ConnectWise apps. Granting these should be rare and always reviewed, and an app granting them to an app is how attackers widen their own access.

## Cortex XDR (XQL)

```sql
config case_sensitive = false
| dataset = msft_azure_ad_audit_raw
| filter activityDisplayName != null
| alter aud_operation = activityDisplayName, aud_initiator_app = json_extract_scalar(initiatedBy, "$.app.displayName"), aud_initiator_user = json_extract_scalar(initiatedBy, "$.user.userPrincipalName"), aud_target_type = json_extract_scalar(targetResources, "$[0].type"), aud_target_name = json_extract_scalar(targetResources, "$[0].displayName"), aud_targets = to_string(targetResources), aud_user_agent = arrayindex(regextract(to_string(additionalDetails), "User-Agent\\\",\\\"value\\\":\\\"([^\\\"]*)"), 0)
| filter aud_operation in ("add app role assignment to service principal", "add app role assignment grant to user", "consent to application") and (aud_targets contains "RoleManagement.ReadWrite.Directory" or aud_targets contains "AppRoleAssignment.ReadWrite.All" or aud_targets contains "Application.ReadWrite.All" or aud_targets contains "Directory.ReadWrite.All" or aud_targets contains "Mail.ReadWrite" or aud_targets contains "Mail.Read" or aud_targets contains "Files.ReadWrite.All" or aud_targets contains "Sites.FullControl.All" or aud_targets contains "full_access_as_app")
| fields _time, aud_operation, aud_initiator_app, aud_initiator_user, aud_target_type, aud_target_name, aud_user_agent, aud_targets
| sort desc _time
| limit 1000
```

## Splunk (SPL, Sysmon)

```
`azure_monitor_aad` category=AuditLogs
| eval aud_operation=operationName, aud_category=category, aud_result='properties.result', aud_initiator_app='properties.initiatedBy.app.displayName', aud_initiator_app_id='properties.initiatedBy.app.appId', aud_initiator_user='properties.initiatedBy.user.userPrincipalName', aud_target_type=mvindex('properties.targetResources{}.type',0), aud_target_name=mvindex('properties.targetResources{}.displayName',0), aud_targets=mvjoin(mvappend('properties.targetResources{}.displayName','properties.targetResources{}.modifiedProperties{}.newValue')," | "), aud_user_agent=mvindex('properties.additionalDetails{}.value',mvfind('properties.additionalDetails{}.key',"User-Agent"))
| where (lower(aud_operation)="add app role assignment to service principal" OR lower(aud_operation)="add app role assignment grant to user" OR lower(aud_operation)="consent to application") AND (match(aud_targets, "(?i)RoleManagement\\.ReadWrite\\.Directory") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)AppRoleAssignment\\.ReadWrite\\.All") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Application\\.ReadWrite\\.All") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Directory\\.ReadWrite\\.All") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Mail\\.ReadWrite") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Mail\\.Read") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Files\\.ReadWrite\\.All") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)Sites\\.FullControl\\.All") OR match(aud_targets, "(?i)full_access_as_app"))
| table _time aud_operation aud_initiator_app aud_initiator_user aud_target_type aud_target_name aud_user_agent aud_targets
| sort - _time
```

## Triage

1. Who granted it (initiator.user or initiator.app) and to which app (target.name)? A grant made by an app, or outside an onboarding ticket, is an escalation.
2. List everything that app can do now (Enterprise applications, Permissions) and check its sign-ins since the grant (DL-0078 pattern).
3. Remove the assignment and revoke the app's credentials if nobody owns the change.

## Known false positives

- Onboarding a new backup, security or migration vendor. These tools really need broad rights. Record the app ID and the business owner when you approve it, then keep this rule for every grant after that.
