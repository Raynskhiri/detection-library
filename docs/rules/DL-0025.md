# DL-0025 · Living-off-the-land binary connects to the internet

**ATT&CK:** [T1105](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1105/), [T1218](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1218/) · **Tactic:** command-and-control · **Severity:** medium · **Data:** `network_connection`

certutil, mshta, regsvr32, rundll32, bitsadmin, a script host, msbuild, installutil, cmstp or odbcconf opens a connection to a public IP. These binaries are signed by Microsoft and are used to download or talk to C2 without dropping a new tool.

## Cortex XDR (XQL)

```sql
config case_sensitive = false
| dataset = xdr_data
| filter event_type = ENUM.NETWORK and action_remote_ip != null
| filter actor_process_image_name in ("certutil.exe", "mshta.exe", "regsvr32.exe", "rundll32.exe", "bitsadmin.exe", "wscript.exe", "cscript.exe", "msbuild.exe", "installutil.exe", "cmstp.exe", "odbcconf.exe", "msxsl.exe", "hh.exe") and not ((action_remote_ip != null and (incidr(action_remote_ip, "10.0.0.0/8") or incidr(action_remote_ip, "172.16.0.0/12") or incidr(action_remote_ip, "192.168.0.0/16") or incidr(action_remote_ip, "127.0.0.0/8") or incidr(action_remote_ip, "169.254.0.0/16") or incidr(action_remote_ip, "100.64.0.0/10"))))
| fields _time, agent_hostname, actor_process_image_name, actor_process_command_line, action_remote_ip, action_remote_port
| sort desc _time
| limit 1000
```

## Splunk (SPL, Sysmon)

```
`sysmon` EventCode=3
| eval process_name=lower(mvindex(split(Image,"\\"),-1))
| where (lower(process_name)="certutil.exe" OR lower(process_name)="mshta.exe" OR lower(process_name)="regsvr32.exe" OR lower(process_name)="rundll32.exe" OR lower(process_name)="bitsadmin.exe" OR lower(process_name)="wscript.exe" OR lower(process_name)="cscript.exe" OR lower(process_name)="msbuild.exe" OR lower(process_name)="installutil.exe" OR lower(process_name)="cmstp.exe" OR lower(process_name)="odbcconf.exe" OR lower(process_name)="msxsl.exe" OR lower(process_name)="hh.exe") AND NOT ((isnotnull(DestinationIp) AND (cidrmatch("10.0.0.0/8", DestinationIp) OR cidrmatch("172.16.0.0/12", DestinationIp) OR cidrmatch("192.168.0.0/16", DestinationIp) OR cidrmatch("127.0.0.0/8", DestinationIp) OR cidrmatch("169.254.0.0/16", DestinationIp) OR cidrmatch("100.64.0.0/10", DestinationIp))))
| table _time host Image DestinationIp DestinationPort
| sort - _time
```

## Triage

1. Resolve the IP (passive DNS) and check its reputation and hosting.
2. Look at the process command line from the matching process start event.

## Known false positives

- rundll32 hosting legitimate control panel or update components that call vendor clouds.
- Proxy setups where every connection goes to a public proxy IP. Exclude the proxy.
- IPv4 only. In XDR, IPv6 peers are in a separate field and are not covered by this rule.
